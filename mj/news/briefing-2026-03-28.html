With a third of global helium supply offline due to the Iran conflict, semiconductor makers are scrambling. Broadcom Warns of AI-Driven Supply Crunch at TSMCBroadcom, TSMC의 AI 수요 공급 부족 경고

CXO Digital Pulse — Mar 2026
Broadcom flagged that TSMC's manufacturing capacity — once considered nearly unlimited — is now maxed out due to surging AI chip demand. Shortages are spreading beyond fabs to PCBs and laser components. Companies are now locking in 3-5 year supply contracts, a dramatic shift from the old just-in-time model. Constraints won't ease until 2027 at the earliest.
