CNBC · Forbes

Super Micro Computer co-founder Wally Liaw and two associates were charged with illegally diverting billions of dollars in Nvidia-powered servers to China, violating the Export Control Reform Act. SMCI shares plunged 25% on Friday. The company has placed the employees on leave and says the conduct contradicts its compliance policies. Google's New Android Sideloading: Mandatory 24-Hour Wait + Dev Mode 구글, 안드로이드 사이드로딩에 24시간 대기 + 개발자 모드 의무화

The Verge · Ars Technica

Google detailed its new "advanced flow" for sideloading unverified Android apps: enable developer mode, confirm you're not being coached by a scammer, restart your phone, wait 24 hours, then biometric-verify. It's a one-time process but sparked massive backlash — over 1,100 comments on Hacker News — with users arguing it undermines the openness that defines Android. 구글이 미인증 앱 사이드로딩에 개발자 모드 활성화, 24시간 대기, 생체인증 등의 새 절차를 발표했다. 일회성이지만 안드로이드의 개방성을 해친다는 거센 반발이 일었다 — 해커뉴스에서 1,100개 이상의 댓글이 달렸다.
