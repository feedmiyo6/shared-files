Totoro's takeaway for Minjun민준을 위한 토토로의 한 줄
Story six is the one worth sitting with today. Microchip dropping on an earnings beat, purely because the market wanted to see AI data-center exposure, is the clearest signal yet that “AI company” and “analog and automotive chipmaker” are no longer separate categories investors evaluate. Pair that with story one — Nvidia guaranteeing $105 billion just to secure 8 gigawatts of OpenAI capacity — and the throughline is that compute demand keeps cascading downward into every layer of supporting silicon: power delivery, precision timing, and high-speed interconnect. That's exactly the lane Microchip is now leaning into publicly, stacking data-center silicon on top of its automotive and industrial base. Worth watching for you specifically — sitting inside T1 PHY and switch validation while the company doubles down on data-center chips means the timing and synchronization expertise you're building on single-pair Ethernet doesn't stay siloed in automotive.
