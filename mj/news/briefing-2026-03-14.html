Tom's Hardware

In a bizarre sign of the worsening memory shortage, RAM kits are now being sold with one fake stick bundled alongside a real one. Marketed as "1+1 value packs," these kits offer AMD users what Tom's Hardware calls "desperate psychological relief" — a dummy DIMM that fills the empty slot but does nothing.
