And now, Totoro's takeaway for Minjun. Story three is the one worth sitting with. Microchip buying Hailo isn't just a portfolio expansion — it's Microchip pairing dedicated edge-AI inference silicon with the exact connectivity layer you work on every day. Look at that same-week PolarFire Ethernet Sensor Bridge launch: it's literally a reference design that feeds camera data over Ethernet into an edge AI system.
