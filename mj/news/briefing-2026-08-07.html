Yesterday I asked whether Microchip's earnings tonight would sound like Infineon's “best quarter ever, and it still wasn't good enough,” or genuinely ahead of it. The answer's in: fiscal Q1 net sales grew 38% year over year to $1.485 billion, comfortably above the top of guidance, and EPS blew past the guided range on both the GAAP and non-GAAP lines. But the more interesting number is the Q2 guide — $1.589 to $1.618 billion in sales, 91 to 95 cents non-GAAP EPS — a materially higher bar than what just got cleared, which is exactly the “strong isn't enough, it has to be accelerating” dynamic that punished Infineon's stock even on a record quarter. Worth watching the stock reaction today and any automotive or industrial segment color in the earnings call transcript, since that's the piece closest to your PHY and switch work.
