1The KO/ZH Unfair Advantage — Press It First한/중 불공정 우위 — 가장 먼저 활용할 것

Highest ROI wedge최고 ROI 지점

Jujube is fully native in English, Korean, and Mandarin — not translated after the fact. Almost no pantry or recipe app on either store is built this way. That means real, physical, walkable, and searchable audiences that most competitors structurally cannot reach. Every dollar and hour spent here has close to zero direct competition. 쥬쥬브는 영어, 한국어, 중국어(만다린)를 완전히 네이티브로 지원합니다 — 나중에 번역한 것이 아닙니다. 두 스토어 어디에도 이런 방식으로 만들어진 팬트리·레시피 앱은 거의 없습니다. 즉, 대부분의 경쟁사가 구조적으로 도달할 수 없는 실질적이고 물리적이며 검색 가능한 사용자층이 존재한다는 뜻입니다. 여기에 쓰는 시간과 비용은 직접적인 경쟁이 거의 없습니다.

4Addendum — With $2,000 Total, Not Monthly부록 — 월 예산이 아니라 총 $2,000일 때

Added 2026-08-06, see companion monetization v3 report2026-08-06 추가, 동반 수익화 v3 보고서 참고

This roadmap's Tier 0 → Tier 3 structure doesn't change — it's already budget-agnostic. What changes is how far $2,000 of one-time capital actually stretches through it, and the honest answer (full math in the monetization v3 report) is: further than you'd think, because the growth this roadmap produces is driven by Weeks 1-4's $0-40/mo Tier 0 actions (KO/ZH ASO, organic content, flyers, community), not by Tier 1-2 paid spend. Concretely: fund Week 1-4 (Tier 0, $0-40) and Week 2's first UGC batch ($225-450) out of pocket regardless — that's under $500 of the $2,000 and is the highest-ROI spend in this entire roadmap.
