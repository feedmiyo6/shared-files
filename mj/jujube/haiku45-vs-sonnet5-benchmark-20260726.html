🎯 Recommendation추천 사항

Hybrid approach. Use Sonnet 5 for the ~700-recipe pool backfill and any recipes marked as "shared" or "featured" (where quality is user-visible and defines the brand). Use Haiku 4.5 for on-demand user recipe generation (unlimited scale, cost-critical).

Full switch to Haiku is technically possible and saves ~66% — but the Korean-native voice gap is real.
