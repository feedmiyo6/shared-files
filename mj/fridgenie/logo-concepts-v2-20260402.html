Concept A

Smart Fridge — Lightbulb

Lightbulb embedded in the lower door = "bright ideas from your fridge." The upper section gets subtle rays suggesting the fridge is "thinking."

Why it works: Universal symbol for intelligence. Reads instantly at any size. The bulb replaces the handle, keeping it minimal.

Concept B

Fridge-Lamp Hybrid — "Genie"

The fridge silhouette subtly morphs into a magic lamp shape at the base, with a spout and wisps. It IS a genie lamp — but it's also unmistakably a fridge.

Why it works: "Fridgenie" = fridge + genie. This makes the name literal in the logo without a cartoon character. The double-read is the whole point.

Concept C

Infinite Loop Handle

The fridge handle becomes a continuous loop flowing through both doors — an infinity symbol turned vertical. Suggests endless possibilities, continuous cycle of cook-store-repeat.

Why it works: Elegant detail that differentiates from generic fridge icons. Works at favicon size. The loop is the only decorative element — everything else stays minimal.

Concept D

Door Ajar — The Glow

Fridge door cracked open with light rays spilling out. That iconic "midnight fridge glow" everyone recognizes. Suggests discovery, magic inside, something waiting for you.

Why it works: Universally relatable moment. The light = intelligence/magic without being literal. Creates depth with minimal perspective shift. This is a story in one icon.

Concept E

Magic Wand Touch

A wand taps the fridge corner with a starburst. Minimal, elegant — "one touch and your fridge becomes magical." Direct genie reference without the character.

Why it works: The wand is a single diagonal line. Zero complexity added but immediately communicates "magic." The sparkle at the tip is the payoff. Works great as app icon and favicon.

Concept F

Friendly Fridge — The Face

Two eyes and a smile on the lower door. Your fridge is alive, friendly, and looking at you. Instant personality, instant brand character without a separate mascot.

Why it works: Character-driven brands stick. Think Duolingo's owl, Notion's emoji. This gives Fridgenie a face people remember. And it's just 5 shapes total.
