🧊 Fridgenie Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name.

Kitchen Inventory Data: Items you add to your fridge, pantry, and freezer inventory. This includes ingredient names, categories, and expiry dates.

Usage Data: Recipe searches, cooking preferences (cuisine preferences, spice level, dietary restrictions), and app interaction patterns.

Voice Recordings: When you use the voice input feature, audio is temporarily recorded on your device, sent to our server for transcription, and immediately deleted. We do not store voice recordings.

Receipt Images: When you scan a receipt, the image is sent to our server for processing and immediately deleted. We do not store receipt images.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage your kitchen inventory and provide recipe suggestions
  • To personalize your experience based on your cooking preferences
  • To process voice input and receipt scans for inventory management
  • To send expiry alerts and cooking suggestions
  • To improve our service and fix bugs

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (hosted on AWS). All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption. We implement row-level security to ensure you can only access your own household's data.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Database and authentication (Privacy Policy)
  • OpenAI: AI-powered recipe suggestions, voice transcription, and receipt scanning. Data sent to OpenAI is processed per their API data usage policy and is not used for training.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with the third-party services listed above as necessary to provide our features.

6. Household Sharing

If you join a household, other members of that household can see the shared inventory. You can leave a household at any time.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data within the app
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data from the Profile screen
  • Export: Contact us to request a copy of your data
  • Correction: You can update your profile and preferences at any time

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Fridgenie is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at fridgenie.app@gmail.com.
