🍃 SnapDigest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SnapDigest collects the following information to provide our service:

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you create an account (or name/email from Apple/Google Sign In).
  • Reading Preferences: Your selected news categories, language preference, and notification settings.
  • Saved Articles: Articles you choose to save for later reading.
  • Device Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens for push notifications (only if you enable notifications).
  • Usage Data: Basic app interaction data to improve the service (no third-party tracking).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalize your news feed based on category preferences
  • Deliver push notifications at your preferred time
  • Provide AI-curated article summaries and translations
  • Save your reading preferences across devices

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Authentication and data storage (hosted in AWS)
  • OpenAI: AI article curation, summarization, and translation (no personal data sent)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Push notification delivery
  • Google AdMob: Advertising (subject to Google's privacy policy)
  • Apple/Google Sign In: Authentication (subject to their respective privacy policies)

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers with row-level security (RLS) enabled. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (TLS) and at rest.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen, which permanently removes all associated data.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: View your data in the app's Settings screen
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all data from Settings → Delete Account
  • Portability: Contact us to request a copy of your data

7. Children's Privacy

SnapDigest is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: support@snapdigest.app
