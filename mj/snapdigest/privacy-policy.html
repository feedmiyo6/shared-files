SnapDigest Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

Effective Date: March 12, 2026

1. Introduction

SnapDigest ("we," "our," or "the app") is an AI-powered daily news digest app. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — used for account creation and authentication
  • Display name — optional, used for personalization
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email as provided by those services

Usage Data

  • Reading history — which articles you read, like, or skip, to personalize your digest
  • Category preferences — your selected news categories
  • Saved articles — articles you bookmark
  • App settings — language, theme, notification preferences

Device Information

  • Push notification token — to send daily digest notifications (if you opt in)
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics to improve the app experience

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and personalize your daily news digest
  • Send push notifications about new digests (if enabled)
  • Improve article curation and recommendations based on your reading patterns
  • Maintain and improve app functionality

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on AWS). We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (TLS) and at rest. Authentication is handled via Supabase Auth with PKCE flow.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Database, authentication, and serverless functions
  • Firebase — Push notifications and analytics
  • OpenAI — AI-powered article summarization and audio generation (processed server-side; no personal data is sent to OpenAI)
  • Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In — Optional authentication providers

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with the service providers listed above as necessary to operate the app.

7. Your Rights

  • Access — You can view your data in the app's Settings
  • Deletion — You can delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete Account
  • Portability — Contact us to request an export of your data
  • Opt-out — You can disable push notifications at any time via your device settings

8. Children's Privacy

SnapDigest is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.
