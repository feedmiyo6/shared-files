📰 SnapDigest Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Email address and display name when you create an account.

Reading Preferences: News categories you select, reading history, saved articles, and interaction patterns (likes, skips, time spent).

Device Information: Device type, OS version, and app version for crash reporting and optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver personalized news content based on your category preferences
  • To generate AI-powered article summaries and audio briefings
  • To improve content recommendations over time based on your reading patterns
  • To send push notifications for your daily briefing (if enabled)
  • To display relevant advertisements
  • To improve our service and fix bugs

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (hosted on AWS). All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption. Row-level security ensures you can only access your own data.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Database and authentication
  • OpenAI: AI-powered article summarization, translation, and text-to-speech. Content sent to OpenAI is processed per their API data usage policy and is not used for training.
  • Google AdMob: Advertising. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase: Push notifications and analytics.
  • RSS Feed Sources: We fetch publicly available RSS feeds from news publishers. No personal data is shared with these sources.

5. Advertising

SnapDigest displays ads via Google AdMob. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's privacy settings. Ad-related data collection is governed by Google's policies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Data is only shared with third-party services listed above as necessary to provide features.

7. Offline Data

Articles and audio you save for offline reading are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

8. Your Rights

  • Access: View all your data within the app
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data from Settings
  • Export: Contact us to request a copy of your data
  • Opt-out: Disable push notifications and personalized ads at any time

9. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. Deleted accounts are permanently removed within 30 days. Cached offline content is removed when you uninstall the app.

10. Children's Privacy

SnapDigest is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified through the app.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at snapdigest.app@gmail.com.
