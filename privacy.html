Fridgenie Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 8, 2026

🌿 Fridgenie respects your privacy. We collect only what's needed to make the app work and never sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Fridgenie, we may collect the following:

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you sign up (via Supabase Auth).
  • Kitchen Data: Pantry items, recipes saved, shopping lists, and household preferences you enter into the app.
  • Voice Input: Audio recordings are processed in real-time for transcription and are not stored after processing.
  • Receipt Images: Photos of receipts are processed for text extraction and are not stored after processing.
  • Usage Data: Basic app usage analytics to improve the experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Fridgenie app experience
  • To suggest recipes based on your ingredients and preferences
  • To manage your household's shared kitchen inventory
  • To process voice commands and receipt scans
  • To improve and optimize the app

3. Third-Party Services

Fridgenie uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Authentication and secure data storage
  • OpenAI: AI-powered recipe suggestions, voice transcription, and receipt scanning (processed via secure server-side functions — no API keys on your device)
  • Google Sign-In: Optional authentication method
  • Amazon Associates: Affiliate links for shopping convenience (no personal data shared)

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) enabled on all tables. This means:

  • You can only access your own household's data
  • All data transmission is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)
  • No API keys or secrets are stored on your device
  • AI processing happens server-side through secure Edge Functions

5. Data Sharing

🔒 We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

Data is only shared with the third-party services listed above, solely for the purpose of providing app functionality.

6. Household Data

When you join or create a household, kitchen data (pantry items, recipes, shopping lists) is shared among household members. You control who joins your household.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app's Profile screen. This will:

  • Permanently delete your account and authentication data
  • Remove your association with any household
  • Delete your personal preferences and saved data

8. Children's Privacy

Fridgenie is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
