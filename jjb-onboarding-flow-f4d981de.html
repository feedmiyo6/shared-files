The onboarding, before it's built

This is the full value-first, hard-paywall welcome flow — every screen a new user sees, in order, from first hello to the paywall. It's a clickable design mock (not app code) so we can lock the flow & sequencing before it goes into Flutter. Read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

The strategy in one line: give one real, bespoke dish free — cooked from their own kitchen — then ask for the subscription. Every wait is turned into a sales moment; every "missing ingredient" becomes a shopping hook. Brand law honored throughout: warm editorial serif, gold hairlines, organic squircle shapes — no pills, no hard rectangles, no green.
